Optimization for Smallholder Farmers

Linear and multi-objective (NSGA-II) optimization balancing cost-effectiveness and nutrition.

  • Multi-objective optimization
  • NSGA-II
  • Food security
Related paper

Applied linear and multi-objective optimization, using SciPy and NSGA-II in pymoo, to decisions facing smallholder farmers. Solutions are ranked by cost-effectiveness and nutritional balance, giving a Pareto front of trade-offs rather than a single answer.

This work connects to our studies of farmer resilience in Senegal and of soft-computing methods for climate adaptation.
