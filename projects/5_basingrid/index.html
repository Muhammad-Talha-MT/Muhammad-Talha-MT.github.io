BASINGRID

A 40-year (1985–2024) dataset of aligned meteorological and landscape rasters for 9,067 U.S. basins.

  • Dataset
  • Geospatial
  • Scientific Data
Paper Code

BASINGRID is a distributed, machine-learning-ready hydrology dataset, published in Scientific Data. For each of 9,067 U.S. basins it provides 40 years (1985–2024) of meteorological forcings and landscape attributes as rasters on a common grid, aligned with streamflow records.

Why distributed

Most large-sample hydrology datasets provide only basin-averaged values. BASINGRID keeps the spatial detail, so models such as HydroTransformer and Basin2Vec can learn where in a basin rain falls and how the landscape responds.
