Now at Oak Ridge National Laboratory

Muhammad Talha

Ph.D. Candidate, Michigan State University Research Intern, Oak Ridge National Laboratory

I am a dual-major Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Biosystems and Agricultural Engineering at Michigan State University, advised by Dr. Amirpouyan Nejadhashemi and Dr. Pang-Ning Tan. I develop foundation models, graph neural networks, and spatiotemporal transformers for hydrologic prediction, including in places where no river gauge exists.

I am currently a Research Intern at Oak Ridge National Laboratory through the Graduate Research Opportunity (GRO) Program, working with Dr. Dan Lu. There I contribute to HydroORBIT, a Genesis Mission–funded multimodal foundation model for continental-scale hydrologic prediction, and build graph neural networks for distributed streamflow prediction.

My dissertation, Spatially Distributed Basin Representations for Hydrologic Prediction Across Space and Time, asks how to learn representations of river basins from distributed meteorological and landscape data that transfer to new places and new time periods.

Research interests

What I work on

Most rivers on Earth have no gauge. I build models that learn from the places we do measure and carry that knowledge to the places we don't.

01

Foundation models for Earth systems

Multimodal pretraining that fuses gauge observations, gridded meteorology, landscape attributes, and National Water Model simulations into one model for continental-scale hydrologic prediction.

  • HydroORBIT

02

Graph learning on river networks

Graph neural networks that pass information along upstream–downstream connectivity, so predictions respect how water actually routes through a basin.

  • GNNs for distributed streamflow

03

Spatiotemporal deep learning

Transformers and CNN–LSTMs that read distributed, gridded inputs directly instead of basin averages, and that generalize to new places and new time periods.

  • HydroTransformer
  • Watershed-agnostic model

04

Transferable basin representations

Embeddings and large aligned datasets that let models trained on gauged basins make predictions in basins they have never seen.

  • Basin2Vec
  • BASINGRID
