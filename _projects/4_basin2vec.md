Basin2Vec learns a compact **embedding for each river basin** from its distributed meteorological and landscape data. Basins with similar hydrologic behavior end up close together in the embedding space, so a model trained on gauged basins can be transferred to **basins it has never seen**.

Accepted at the IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV 2027).
