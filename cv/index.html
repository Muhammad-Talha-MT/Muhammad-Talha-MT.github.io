Profile

Dual-major Ph.D. candidate developing AI for Earth-system prediction through multimodal foundation models, graph neural networks, spatiotemporal transformers, and transferable representations. At Oak Ridge National Laboratory, contributing to the Genesis-funded HydroORBIT project and graph-based hydrologic modeling. Experience integrating environmental observations, geospatial attributes, and numerical-model simulations.

Experience

  1. Aug 2026 – Present

    Research Intern, Graduate Research Opportunity (GRO) Program

    Oak Ridge National Laboratory · Oak Ridge, Tennessee

    Mentor: Dr. Dan Lu

    • Contributing to HydroORBIT, a Genesis-funded multimodal foundation model for continental-scale hydrologic prediction, integrating environmental observations and simulations.
    • Developing graph neural networks for distributed streamflow prediction using river-network connectivity, NWM pretraining, and gauge observations, with benchmarking against neural models and TVA forecasts.
    • Building aligned multimodal datasets from gridded fields, basin attributes, and gauge time series, including point-cloud representations for spatial and temporal synchronization.

  2. Jan 2023 – Jul 2026

    Graduate Research Assistant

    Michigan State University · East Lansing, Michigan

    Advisors: Dr. Amirpouyan Nejadhashemi and Dr. Pang-Ning Tan

    • Developed HydroTransformer and an explainable watershed-agnostic CNN–LSTM for streamflow prediction from distributed inputs, evaluating generalization across space and time.
    • Developed Basin2Vec, a basin-centric representation-learning framework for transferable hydrologic prediction in previously unseen basins.
    • Created BASINGRID, a 40-year (1985–2024) dataset of aligned meteorological and landscape rasters covering 9,067 U.S. basins.

  3. Jun 2021 – Dec 2022

    Machine Learning Engineer

    Ibex (TRG)

    • Developed a real-time compliance system processing over 20,000 agent screen recordings using CNN-based form detection and OCR-based information extraction.
